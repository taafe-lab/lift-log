# Lift Log

A push, pull and legs training log. It runs on the iPhone Home Screen and works with no internet. Workouts you log are stored on your phone, not here.

## Put it on your iPhone

1. Open `https://taafe-lab.github.io/lift-log/` in Safari.
2. Tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Open it from the Home Screen once while you have signal. You'll see "Saved to this phone. It now works offline."

Always use the Home Screen icon. Safari keeps a separate copy of the data.

## Back up your training

Settings, **Save a backup**, then **Save to Files** and pick iCloud Drive. Do it every couple of weeks, and before you change phones. The app reminds you on the home screen when one is due.

## New phone

Put the app on the new phone as above, then Settings, **Restore from a backup**, and pick the newest backup file.

## Changes and updates

Small changes to your programme (sets, exercises, rest times, notes) you make yourself in the app with **Edit** on any workout.

For changes to the app itself, ask Claude in your Workout Log project. You'll see screenshots first, and it's only published here once you say yes. The next time you open the app with signal it says a new version is ready; tap **Reload**. Your logged workouts are not touched.

The `source` folder holds the code the app is built from, and `CLAUDE.md` holds the full technical notes.
